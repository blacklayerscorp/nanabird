How it works

Three steps from “hello” to “hello, Nani”

No flashcards to sort out or curriculum to choose. Just introduce your child, and Tilo takes it from there.

  1. 1

    Introduce your child to Tilo

    Tilo starts by asking you, the grown-up, to say hello and introduce your child in whatever language feels natural. Speak Punjabi, Urdu, Arabic or a mix, and Tilo switches to match you. Then it turns to your child and makes a new friend.

    “Tilo, this is Zara. She's seven, she loves cats, and her Teta lives in Amman.”

  2. 2

    Talk, listen, read, trace

    Short lessons start with speaking out loud, because that's how children learn a language at home. As confidence grows, Tilo adds listening games, matching words to their script, and tracing letters with a fingertip.

    Lessons take about 5 to 8 minutes, and you set the daily limit.

  3. 3

    Watch them grow in Nanabird Parents

    On your own phone, see minutes, streaks, every new word in its native script, and a two-line summary after each lesson. It's a quiet window into their progress, without having to hover.

    The kids app has no dashboards and nothing to buy. That all lives with you.

Voice first

It feels like a chat with a friend, not a worksheet.

Children learn to speak by speaking. So Nanabird puts talking first. Tilo asks, listens, cheers, and gently helps with tricky sounds, using the words your family actually says at home.

  • Real dialects, not just textbook language. Levantine or Egyptian Arabic, Mexican Spanish, Majhi Punjabi. You choose for each child.
  • Tilo remembers your child: their buddy, their interests and who speaks the language at home, so lessons feel personal.
  • Honest about being an AI. Tilo tells children it's an AI, stays on the lesson and never asks for personal details.

Four skills, one friend

Speaking first, then everything else

Nanabird follows the way children pick up a language at home: listen and talk first, then connect sounds to letters on the page.

Speaking

Tilo says a word, your child says it back, and Tilo listens and helps with the sounds that don't exist in English.

“Now you! ਪਾਣੀ, paani. Water!”

Listening

Playful back-and-forth where your child listens for words they know and answers Tilo, a little more each day.

“Did you hear lola in that? Where?”

Reading

Children match the words they hear to the same words in their own script, building early reading one familiar word at a time.

Which one says نانی?

Writing

Fingertip letter tracing in 7 alphabets: Gurmukhi, Shahmukhi, Urdu, Arabic, Devanagari, and the Tagalog and Spanish alphabets.

Example letters: ਕکकبNg

Family missions

Lessons that end at the dinner table

After a lesson, Tilo gives your child a tiny mission to do with someone they love. Practice becomes a moment with Nani, Dadi, Teta or Lola. Every finished mission earns a stamp in your child's language passport.

  • Grandparents become the experts. Their way of saying it is the right way for your family.
  • Grandparents far away? Try the mission on your next video call.
  • No pressure. Missions are invitations, never homework.

Safety & privacy

Built for children from the very first line of code

These are promises, not settings you have to find. Lessons are powered by Google's Gemini on Google Cloud, which we've configured not to use your child's data to train models.

  • No ads. Ever.

    No ads, no in-lesson upsells and no sponsored content in the kids app. Purchases live only in the parents' app.

  • No tracking or analytics

    No third-party analytics, no advertising identifier, no location, contacts or photos. This website doesn't use cookies either.

  • Voice is never stored

    Your child's voice streams only while a lesson is open so Tilo can answer, and is discarded when it ends.

  • Transcripts are never stored

    Lesson text is held in memory just long enough to write your summary, then deleted. It is never written to disk.

  • Parents hold the keys

    Children never create accounts. You sign in with Apple, give consent and can review or change anything.

  • Delete everything, anytime

    Remove one child or the whole family from Nanabird Parents. It's gone from our servers immediately, and from backups within 30 days.

  • Tracing stays on the device

    Letter-tracing strokes never leave your child's iPad. Only the score is saved.

  • Safe by design

    Tilo stays on lessons and never asks for personal details. If something worrying comes up, it gently points to a trusted grown-up and alerts you, recording only the category of concern, never the words.

FAQ

Questions parents ask us

Who is Nanabird for?

Children aged 5 to 12 whose families want to keep a heritage language alive: kids who understand Nani but answer in English, kids who have never heard their grandparents' language at school, and everyone in between. A parent or guardian sets everything up and stays in charge.

I don't speak the language well myself. Can we still use it?

Yes. Tilo does the teaching, so you don't need to be fluent. Many families use Nanabird because the language skipped a generation. The family missions are designed for exactly this: they bring grandparents and relatives in as the experts.

Which languages and dialects are included?

Seven languages have curated word packs, written per dialect with native script and romanization and reviewed by native speakers: Punjabi (Gurmukhi), Urdu, Hindi, Levantine Arabic, Egyptian Arabic, Tagalog and Mexican Spanish. Around 28 more languages are taught live by Tilo. Those are clearly marked “AI-taught, not reviewed” so you always know the difference.

Is Tilo a real person?

No. Tilo is an AI voice powered by Google's Gemini, and it tells children so. Tilo stays on lessons, never asks for personal details and blocks unsafe content. Like any AI, it can make mistakes, including with words and pronunciation. That's why the core word packs are reviewed by native speakers.

Do you record my child's voice?

No. Your child's voice streams through our server to Google Cloud only while a lesson is open, so Tilo can hear and answer. It is never stored, and neither is the transcript. The transcript is held in memory to write your two-line summary, then deleted when the lesson ends. Read the full Privacy Policy.

How much screen time is this?

Lessons are short, about 5 to 8 minutes, and mostly talking and listening rather than tapping. There's a daily limit you control from Nanabird Parents. It's 20 minutes by default, and you can change it for each child.

What do we need to use it?

An iPhone or iPad with iOS 18 or later for your child, and Nanabird Parents on your own iPhone. You link them with a 6-digit code. Nanabird is coming soon to the App Store. There is no Android version yet.

How do the free minutes and minute packs work?

Every family can try 15 free minutes with Tilo. After that, a grown-up buys a minute pack in the app: a one-time purchase, not a subscription. Minutes never expire, are shared by every child and device in the family, and are only counted while Tilo is talking.
